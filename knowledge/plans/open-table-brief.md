# Open Table

Scope: Approved Home and reading-page adaptation, extended through the shared site shell and existing secondary pages. Steve approved both representative previews before implementation. Visitor mode: Read, with project exploration alongside the writing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal welcome connects real software to the thinking behind it. Replace the old animated gradient banner and decorated cards with a clear introduction and an uncropped project spread.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream `#fbf4e8`, dark ink `#292720`, brick `#9c422c`, quiet rules `#cfc5b5`. Source Sans 3 carries the main typography; Bitter gives the personal note and quotations a separate voice. No decorative glows or card stripes.

STORY: Meet Steve, see Rachel, Code198x and Chaotic Tanx with honest statuses, then explore the writing. Code198x covers programming, game-making and computing history; follow `RULES.md`.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On desktop, a compact masthead sits over a broad left-hand welcome and right-hand introduction, then three real project images and a compact writing index. Articles show a clear title/date, a roughly 70ch reading measure and contents in the margin. On mobile, contents open above the prose using native disclosure; navigation never requires animation.

FORM: Open Table, grounded candidate 7, direction seed `0c6a94b1`, approved by Steve in conversation. Code-led browser sketches are the session's review medium; no permanent workflow default is assumed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
