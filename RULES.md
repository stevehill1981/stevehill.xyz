# Project rules

## Code198x scope

Describe Code198x broadly; it includes programming foundations, languages beyond assembly, game craft and computing history. Never reduce it to an assembly-only course. This is a content rule, not a restriction on articles about assembly.

Confirmed by Steve on 15 October 2026 after correcting the narrower homepage description.

## Code198x name

Call it "Code Like It's 198x" in site pages, navigation, labels and project copy, matching the Code198x site's own title. "Code198x" stays acceptable in URLs, IDs and the domain. Published blog posts keep their original wording.

Chosen by Steve on 16 October 2026 after the Impeccable critique found the two names used as if they were different things.
