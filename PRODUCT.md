## Platform

## Users

Readers and fellow developers are the primary audience, confirmed by Steve on 15 October 2026. Prospective collaborators should still be able to find his experience and contact details.

## Product Purpose

Steve Hill's personal website connects his engineering writing, independent projects and professional experience. It should remain useful between posts; Steve posts infrequently and spends much of his time building projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Astro static site deployed to GitHub Pages by CI on pushes to main. Never run a manual deploy command.
- Preserve published URLs, content, RSS, search, project destinations, series navigation and offline behavior through the redesign.
- Preserve the agreed homepage order: short introduction, selected projects, recent writing.
- Work With Me remains selectively available and linked from About, deliberately outside the top navigation, per `knowledge/log.md`.
- No new dependencies, API/schema breaking changes or architectural patterns without approval.

## Brand Commitments

- Steve confirmed a warm, personal direction for the whole-site redesign.
- Steve approved the Open Table browser preview on 15 October 2026: warm cream, dark ink and brick, a personal introduction, real project images and a restrained writing index. Steve also approved the matching article adaptation before the whole-site rollout.
- This remains Steve's personal site. Phosphene is his publishing brand for independent software and games, with Steve publicly identifiable as creator; current App Store releases appear under his own name. Use Phosphene, not Phosphene Studios; destination: https://phosphene.uk.
- Code198x is live at https://www.code198x.com; the wider 198x family is under active development. Phosphene and 198x retain their own identities.
- Code198x includes programming foundations, languages beyond assembly, game craft and computing history. Follow `RULES.md`; Steve approved the broader description and rule on 15 October 2026.
- Preserve direct, first-person writing and factual project claims. Do not invent testimonials, customers or metrics.

- Rachel is released on the App Store; Chaotic Tanx is paused pending refactoring for performance and playability; Code198x is live. Confirmations and verification are in `knowledge/plans/project-availability.md`.

## Product Principles

- Make the writing comfortable to read and easy to explore.
- Show the real work and its current availability.
- Make the site useful without requiring a frequent posting schedule.
- Keep personal identity and product brands clearly connected without conflating them.

## Open Decisions
